About me

The Journey

As a Field Producer and Video Journalist, I have covered major European political and economic events - from high-level diplomatic meetings and international conferences to major sprot events. My primary expertise is live production, camera and editing, taking responsibilities to ensure smooth and successful coverage. This included organizing logistics, coordinating teams, securing accreditations and access, and arranging interviews with senior officials, from UN Secretaries-General to European Commissioners and heads of state.

Through collaboration with hundreds, of colleagues on highly demanding assignments, I have learned that the best results come from an atmosphere of mutual trust, respect, and teamwork. Combining technical expertise with editorial and production skills, I work with speed, precision, and reliability.
