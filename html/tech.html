1 THE PROBLEM

THE REAL PROBLEM IS THE NETWORK

Live streaming rarely fails because of cameras or codecs.
It fails because internet connections on location are unstable, shared and unpredictable.

2 THE METHOD

Multiple internet sources are used simultaneously.

ETHERNET, WIFI AND CELLULAR LINKS ARE COMBINED AND BALANCED IN REAL TIME.

If one degrades or drops, the others keep the signal alive.

No manual switching.

No restarting the stream.

THE SYSTEM REACTS AUTOMATICALLY,
WITHOUT INTERRUPTING THE LIVE.

Congestion, fluctuating bandwidth, packet loss and sudden drops are normal field conditions.
